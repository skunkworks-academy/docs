## Documentation domains

- **Learners and instructors:** course access, labs, assessments, and support guidance.
- **Content teams:** authoring, review, publishing, versioning, and maintenance standards.
- **Engineering teams:** architecture, deployment, identity, security, observability, and integrations.
- **Operations teams:** service ownership, incident handling, data governance, and lifecycle management.
